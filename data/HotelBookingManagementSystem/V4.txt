Business travellers use HMBS to reserve special accommodation offers from participating hotels. Registration with HBMS requires their name and billing details, including a company name and address. They may also supply travel preferences, such as included breakfast, free wifi, or a reception desk open 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

To look for accommodation, a traveller enters a city, arrival and departure dates, the number and types of rooms required—single, double, or twin, for example—a minimum star rating, and an approximate budget expressed as the maximum nightly cost. Additional preferences may optionally narrow the search. HBMS presents all available hotel offers for that period. The traveller can make a preliminary booking or follow the ordinary process to complete one.

For a preliminary booking, HBMS distributes key booking details to other hotels: price, city area, hotel rating, key preferences, and a unique booking identifier. Those hotels have the next 24 hours to compete by submitting special offers. When that period ends, HBMS sends the traveller the five best offers. The traveller may choose a new offer or continue with the initial preliminary booking.

Either route requires credit card details to finalize the booking. Finalized bookings may be prepaid, meaning immediate, non-reimbursable payment, or paid at the hotel during the stay. The hotel must confirm a finalized booking within 24 hours. A booking can include a cancellation deadline. Cancelling a confirmed booking before that deadline has no further consequences; cancelling after it incurs a charge for one night's accommodation. HBMS retains each traveller's entire past booking information to compute a reliability rating.

A hotel has a specific address in a city and may be operated by a hotel chain. Hotels can publish the room types they have available for a given period in HBMS and can report when a room type has sold out. HBMS passes preliminary booking information to competing hotels along with the traveller's preferences and reliability rating, enabling them to submit special offers.

Once a booking is finalized, the hotel must send the traveller a confirmation. If it fails to confirm a completed booking within 24 hours, HBMS must cancel it and refund the traveller if it was prepaid. A hotel that needs to cancel an already confirmed booking must offer the traveller financial compensation.
