Helping Hands Store (H2S) gathers used articles and non-perishable food from city residents and supplies them to people in need. It operates in many cities, with each location independently run. To expand the supply of items, H2S wants to offer customers a Pickup and Delivery Service. Through the H2S website, residents would be able to arrange collection from a street address.

A resident supplies a name, street address, telephone number, a description of the items for collection, and, optionally, an email address. On the morning of the requested weekday, the resident leaves the items immediately outside the building's front door at the specified address.

H2S collects these items with its fleet of pickup vehicles. Every weekday begins with a route being set for each vehicle that has an available volunteer driver. Volunteers enter their available days on the website. Route planning considers the vehicle's storage space and the scheduled items' sizes and weights. A scheduled collection can take place at any time from 8:00 to 14:00. After finishing the scheduled pickups, the driver takes all collected second-hand articles to the H2S distribution center. Non-perishable food goes directly to the food bank instead, and the food bank handles it without further H2S involvement.

An H2S employee at the distribution center checks the quality of arriving second-hand articles. Articles that remain usable receive RFID tags. The employee verifies the resident's description and corrects it where necessary, then categorizes the article using a standard list of 134 categories, including baby clothing, women's winter boots, fridges, and microwaves.

Distribution centers in some H2S cities also serve recipients who cannot come to the center themselves. These clients can specify the categories of articles they need. Each evening, an H2S employee telephones them about relevant articles received that day. If a client still requires an article, the employee organizes delivery to the client's home address. A volunteer driver carries out such deliveries before collecting items along the pickup route.
