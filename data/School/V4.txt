Every school has a name. Teachers work at schools, and students attend classes. Both students and teachers have their first names and last names recorded. Teachers' email addresses are known as well. Each student attends precisely one school. A teacher may teach at no more than three schools, and the number of teaching hours is known for each such teacher–school relationship. Every school has exactly one principal, selected from among the teachers. Although teachers may teach at several schools, a teacher can be principal of at most one school.

Along with the people working there, a school consists of many rooms. These may be classrooms, which have a name and a capacity, or other rooms, such as a gym, which have a name and a size specified in square meters. Every class group has a name, for example 5a, and is formed by between 10 and 31 students. Each student always belongs to exactly one class group. Every class group is assigned exactly one classroom, and every classroom is assigned exactly one class group.
