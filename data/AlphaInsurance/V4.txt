Alpha Insurance offers its clients a range of insurance policy types.

When a customer first approaches the company, a broker is allocated to handle that customer's file. The system records the broker, allowing the help desk to identify the customer's first account manager immediately from the contract when the customer telephones. Once this allocation has taken place, the customer states the type or types of policy they wish to take out. Depending on the situation, the broker then evaluates the customer's profile directly or forwards the file to head office for analysis. If the assessment finds the customer trustworthy, a preliminary contract or offer for an insurance product is presented in person or emailed. Existing customers may receive these offers too. Acceptance leads to both parties signing the contract. Following signature, the client receives coverage and is billed at the price of the purchased insurance product. The contract specifies the client's choice of monthly or yearly invoicing.

When an insured event occurs, the customer should submit a compensation claim. The company responds by creating one or more claim cases. For example, material and physical damage from an accident are often processed separately. A completed case file goes to different estimators for assessment according to their expertise. Their reports form the basis for deciding whether to approve the claim case. If it is approved, a compensation decision is recorded, specifying the costs that qualify for full or partial reimbursement. The compensation amount is computed for the documents supplied and transferred to the customer's account. Legal requirements mean that the estimators' reports must remain in the database for no less than one year following payment of compensation.
