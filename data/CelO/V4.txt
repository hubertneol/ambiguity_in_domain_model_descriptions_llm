Celebrations Organization System
CelO assists families and groups of friends in planning birthday celebrations and other events. It allows organizers to monitor attendance and task completion, while attendees can state what they intend to bring.

Small events normally have one organizer; large events need several. An organizer supplies a first name, last name, email address, postal address, phone number, and password. The email address doubles as the username. The organizer specifies the event type by choosing an existing option, such as a birthday or graduation party, or defining a new type. They must also give the event's starting and ending dates and times, occasion, and location. A location may be chosen from an existing list or created by entering its name and address. To invite attendees, the organizer enters their first names, last names, and email addresses. Organizing and attending do not always coincide: an organizer may manage an event without attending it, or may attend as well.

On receiving an invitation by email, an attendee who lacks an account can create one. They choose a new password and use the invitation's email address as their username. They can then respond that they will come, might come, or cannot come. The organizer can inspect the event's invitation status, including counts of replies and outstanding replies and the people who will definitely or possibly attend.

Selecting an event displays its specific checklist to the organizer. Bringing a birthday cake is one possible birthday-party task. The organizer can mark each checklist task as still needing completion, completed, or inapplicable to that event.

Organizers may extend the checklist with tasks that will also appear for the next event. For instance, adding a task to bring birthday candles for a birthday party makes it available at the following birthday party as well. An organizer may designate checklist tasks for attendees, such as bringing the cake. When tasks are delegated this way, attendees who have confirmed that they will attend see the list of tasks for attendees to carry out. They can select their own tasks, letting the organizer see who will bring each item.
