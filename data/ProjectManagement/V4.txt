This case concerns the management of research projects. A university comprises many research groups spread across different departments. Every researcher belongs to one research group only. A project is divided into Work Packages (WPs), each led by exactly one assigned research group. Researchers must be assigned to WPs to carry out a project.

The group leading a WP does not limit who may work on it: a researcher can be assigned to any WP.

Work supplied by one group to another must nevertheless be traceable as service delivery. It must be possible to see which researchers from other groups work on the WPs that a given research group leads. To maintain accurate service-delivery records when a researcher moves to another group, their existing WP team memberships are ended and replacement assignments are created. A change in a WP's leading group similarly requires the team-member assignments to be recorded anew, keeping the service-delivery history correct.
