A gas station chain plans to manage its stations electronically. Use the following requirements to represent the situation in a UML class diagram.

Every station records an address and a unique ID. It contains fuel pumps, up to a maximum of twelve. A number identifies each pump, and a flag records whether the pump is self-service. The fuels available from each pump are known. Fuel names provide unique identification, and each fuel's octane rating is recorded.

Every station keeps an archive of all its daily fuel prices. The combination of date, applicable fuel, and station identifies a daily price, which also records the price for a liter. Fuel purchases have unique IDs. For a purchase, records show the pump used, the fuel bought, and the daily price applied. The volume dispensed in liters is recorded as well.
