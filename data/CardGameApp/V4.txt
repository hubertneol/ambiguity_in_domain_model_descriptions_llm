You are designing an app for German clubs to play a card game and are now examining the system's data structure. The customer has supplied this specification.

Four players, a card deck, and a scoring table make up a game. A deck may contain no more than 48 cards. Each card provides a method that compares card height and yields one of three results: lower, equal, or higher. The available suits are hearts, diamonds, clubs, and spades.

The scoring table can hold any number of rounds. Every round records the players who won and the points value. Jack rounds and solo rounds are not mutually exclusive: the same round may be both. A mandatory solo round is a particular form of solo round. Every solo round has one solo type—trump, jack, or queen—and precisely one player acting as the solo player.

All objects carry a unique ID attribute so that they can be located.
