There is no fixed limit on how many customers a bank manages or how many branches make up a bank. Every customer's given name and surname are recorded as Strings. Customers hold between one and five accounts, inclusive. The owner of every account is one of the bank's customers. Accounts fall into two basic categories: savings accounts and current accounts. All accounts record an int account number and a double current balance. A current account additionally records its overdraft limit as a double.
