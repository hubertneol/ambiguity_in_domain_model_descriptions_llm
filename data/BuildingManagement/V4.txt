Project description:
A web portal records a version number and a URL, for example “http://www.ebuntu-nrw.org”. It makes available a building catalog containing energy information. Each building is identified by a unique ID and has a name, address, construction year, and energy class selected from A, B, C, and D. A user is designated as its owner and may supply extra entries for the building. Every extra entry is named. Its content is either text or a floating-point value accompanied by a unit, with the unit expressed as freely definable text. For each entry, the owner may choose to make it public, allowing even visitors who have not registered with that portal to see it. Buildings also have a collection of images, exactly one of which serves as the profile photo. A file name is stored for each image.

To register as a portal user, a visitor supplies a unique user name, an email address, and a password. Registration includes a uniqueness check on the user name and validation of the email address through a confirmation link. Meanwhile, an administrator sets up the user for the portal. Completion of these steps triggers an automatic activation email to the user, who can then log in. Logged-in users may create buildings, maintain entries for their own buildings, and post comments on buildings belonging to other users. A full-text search lets any site visitor find relevant buildings in the system. Administrators constitute a special category of user, with an ID card number recorded in addition to the usual user details. Their administrative responsibilities include creating user accounts and removing comments.
