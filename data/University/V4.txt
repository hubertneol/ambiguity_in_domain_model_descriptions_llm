A university comprises several faculties, each containing various institutes. Both faculties and institutes have names, and institute addresses are known. A dean who is employed by the university heads each faculty.

The university's total employee count is known. Employees have names, social security numbers, and email addresses, and are divided into research and administrative staff.

Each research assistant (RA) is assigned to one or more institutes, and their research field is known. RAs may also spend a specified number of hours on projects. Project names and starting and ending dates are known. RAs who teach courses are called lecturers.

Every course has a unique numeric ID, a name, and a weekly duration expressed in hours.
