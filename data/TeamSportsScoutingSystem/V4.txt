Club employees including scouts, the head coach, and the director use the Team Sports Scouting System. The head coach defines desired profiles for future player signings. A profile specifies target playing positions, such as GK for goalkeeper or LB for left back, together with other player attributes identified by name and value.

Whenever scouts notice players who appear to fit a target profile, they can add them to a long list. The head scout reviews this list periodically while assigning the team to investigate individual players in greater depth. Completing an assignment entails submitting a report on the player. The report sets out strengths and weaknesses and a recommendation, such as key player, first team player, reserve team player, prospective player, or not a good signing.

After considering the initial scouting results for a target profile, the head coach and head scout choose the players to place on the short list. Short-listed players may undergo several further rounds through scouting assignments, including some performed personally by the head scout. Once the head scout ultimately recommends a player for signing, the director issues an official offer for that player.
